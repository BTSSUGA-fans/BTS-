# BTS-
BTS (Bangtan Sonyeondan), also known as the Bangtan Boys or Beyond the Scene, is a globally acclaimed seven-member South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013. Consisting of Jin, Suga, J-Hope, RM, Jimin, V, and Jungkook, they are known for co-writing/producing their music, which covers topics like mental health, self-love.
